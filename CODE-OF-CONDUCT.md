# Code of Conduct

1. Respect contributors regardless of cohort or seniority.
2. Criticize ideas and work, not people.
3. Do not claim ownership over collective knowledge.
4. Document work that others may need to continue.
5. Do not upload credentials, secrets, or private information.
6. Give credit to original authors.
7. Keep repositories understandable and reproducible.
8. Leave the space better than you found it.
